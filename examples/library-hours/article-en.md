# Two more hours at the library

An invented library notice offers evening readers a specific change: Friday closing will move from 6 p.m. to 8 p.m. during an approved four-week trial. The trial has not started, and the number of seats will stay the same. [S1]

The extra time is useful in its own right. What interests me is whether it will also change when people arrive. The notice gives us no crowding figures, so it cannot tell us whether readers will spread out across the longer evening or arrive together.

I'd look at demand after the trial before describing the room as less crowded. For now, the plan promises a longer Friday visit, not an easier seat search.
