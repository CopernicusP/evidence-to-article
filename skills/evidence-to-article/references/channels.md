# Channel composition

Read only the requested language sections. Use task/profile lengths. When unspecified, choose a useful scope and state it; never invent a company requirement.

## Chinese explanatory article

Write from the Chinese reader's understanding order. Start with the actual event, choice, document or consequence. Introduce background when it explains that event. Use specific declarative subheadings by default, with no artificial equal-length pattern or empty suspense. Let evidence determine section length.

Translate relationships and meaning, not English sentence order. Say who submitted a document, who signed an agreement and who received money before stacking institutional labels. Explain a necessary technical term when it becomes useful. Keep legal qualifications near the claims. End when the opening problem is resolved, without an additional abstract moral.

Use ordinary text weight by default. Bold only for navigation or a necessary distinction. A common transition word is not itself an error; remove it when it adds no relationship or information. Keep sources and required disclaimers separate from body-length measurements.

Separate the reader's explanation from the editor's work log. State the factual distinction directly instead of writing “不能写成”“本文选择这样处理” or explaining how the draft was checked. Keep qualifications that change the claim beside it; consolidate detailed access limits, conflicting source endpoints and method notes below the article. Human/agent review status belongs in the delivery record, not the reading article. Before introducing a new legal or technical process, add the short sourced bridge a non-specialist needs to distinguish it from the previous one.

## English blog

Lead with the concrete development or decision giving the article a purpose. Develop one argument with connected paragraphs. Definitions should explain an event already on the page; avoid an opening inventory of acronyms, identifiers and categories.

Use headings that carry the explanation forward. Do not force background, regulation, stakeholders and outlook into separate slots. Keep precise distinctions and attribution; move lookup identifiers to source notes when they interrupt the argument. Spend space on the decisive misunderstanding or mechanism, not every fact found in research.

Deliver the complete article first, then supporting notes. Notes are outside the blog's length target unless the brief says otherwise. Do not reproduce a research checklist as prose. Use concrete, proportionate language without quotas of jokes, questions, metaphors or short sentences.

Explain the subject rather than narrating the drafting process. Preserve the meaning of an evidence limit, but put detailed source-access and editorial-method commentary in source notes and review status in the run record. When moving to a different technical process, define the relevant distinction before drawing its implication. Apply these as passage-level judgments, not a ban on useful authorial transparency.

## Optional audio handoff

Generate audio only when requested and the permitted provider is known. Use the checked article and claim packet as input; preserve their version. Give both hosts useful evidence, explanations or follow-up questions. Do not make one host permanently uninformed.

Review what was actually spoken. A generative overview can add claims or change numerical relationships even when the script is correct. Keep recording script and actual transcript separately named. Do not label text review as listening or a substituted provider as the requested provider. Organization-specific production belongs in the organization profile.
