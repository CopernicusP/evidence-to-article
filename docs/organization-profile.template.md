# Organization profile template

Copy this template into a private location and fill only the fields relevant
to the current task. This empty template contains no organization's policy.
The executing agent must receive the selected profile explicitly; the helper
scripts do not load it automatically.

- **Profile name and version:**
- **Scope:** which organization, audience, channel, and task types this covers.
- **Current owner and approval status:** distinguish approved requirements from
  proposed preferences; do not invent an approval.
- **Reader and editorial purpose:** what the reader should understand or decide.
- **Outputs:** languages, formats, body-count conventions and bounds, and any
  separately checked rendered-page target.
- **Core dependency:** Evidence to Article version, repository and tested commit; record the actual loaded version for each run. Reference the core rather than copying its rules.
- **Voice and structure:** select neutral explainer, grounded personal, institutional commentary, or a supplied mode; name the narrator and the boundaries. First-person analysis does not authorize invented experience or institutional positions.
- **Reader feedback:** exact artifact, date, speaker and approved scope; keep a personal preference separate from organization approval and measured editing-time improvement.
- **Terminology:** preferred names, translations, and definitions.
- **Required qualifications:** supplied legal or editorial text, its applicable
  scope, and where it belongs. Do not create organization policy from a guess.
- **Evidence standards:** acceptable source types, cutoff dates, and treatment
  of inaccessible or conflicting evidence.
- **Approved examples:** private locators, exact approval scope, and the
  technique to learn. An example is not evidence for a new article's claims.
- **Revision input:** how to identify the editor's reviewed version and locate its comments. Record unavailable feedback separately from independently proposed edits.
- **Review and publication:** which criteria apply to this channel, what evidence reviewers need, and which output formats they inspect. Assign each active criterion its own reviewer and a separate reviewer to the revised whole article. Record actual human reading and publication approvals with their version and authorized delivery destination.
- **Exclusions:** other clients, channels, past episodes, or one-off preferences
  that must not influence this run.

Freeze the selected version in each run brief. Keep source documents, client
drafts, credentials, reviewer identities, and episode-specific decisions with
that private run, outside the reusable public core.
